What we do

Four engineering disciplines. One point of responsibility.

From excavation, foundations and structural frames to roads and planting, one team owns your project end to end, wherever it is in the Kingdom.

01

Civil construction

Solid structures start with the right foundation. From site preparation and excavation to the completed concrete frame, we deliver civil works with qualified field crews, direct engineering supervision and QA hold points at every stage. 02

Roads & infrastructure

Roads that serve the city for decades. Field experience on major road corridors, from cutting and grading to stormwater drainage, sidewalks, curbs and lighting ducts, delivered with traffic flow and road-user safety in mind, anywhere in the Kingdom. 03

Structural strengthening

Extending a building's life with engineering confidence. We restore the strength of existing buildings with engineered solutions that start with a precise assessment: foundations, columns and slabs, steel strengthening, and new slabs built with steel doweling. 04

Landscaping & irrigation

Sustainable green in the heart of the city. Green spaces built for the Kingdom's hot climate: we select the right trees, design water-saving irrigation networks and maintain them so they stay at their best all year.

    FAQ

    Before we start together

    What services does CIC offer?

    Four core disciplines across Saudi Arabia: civil construction (excavation, foundations, concrete frames), roads and infrastructure, structural strengthening and restoration, and landscaping with irrigation networks.

    Do you work across Saudi Arabia or only in Madinah?

    Across the Kingdom. We are headquartered in Madinah (Al-Ranuna district), and our work outside it includes building infrastructure at Jazan Port to Aramco ports specifications.

    How do I choose a reliable contractor in Saudi Arabia?

    Check a valid CR and VAT number, completed projects you can see, an itemised offer with quantities rather than a lump sum, and engineering supervision with regular reports. CIC is registered under CR 4650273835, VAT 312325975900003.

    How much does structural strengthening or a concrete frame cost in KSA?

    It depends on area, floors, soil or building condition and the strengthening method, so we don't quote blind. We start with a site visit, then send an itemised technical and financial offer.

    How do I get a quote?

    Send your project type, location, approximate area and photos on WhatsApp (+966 53 452 5202). We arrange a site visit, then send a detailed offer.

    What are the signs a building needs strengthening?

    Diagonal or widening cracks, tilting or settlement, spalling concrete with exposed rebar, fire or explosion damage, or plans to add floors and loads. We always start with an engineering assessment.

    Can you strengthen an existing building without demolishing it?

    In most cases, yes. We have underpinned foundations to stop a building tilting, added steel strengthening to columns and slabs, and built intermediate slabs with steel doweling, all without demolition.

    Do you deliver public roads and infrastructure?

    Yes, including the King Abdullah Road upgrade, completion of Prince Nayef Road, the King Salman Road walkway, stormwater drainage, sidewalks, curbs and lighting ducts.
